Mechanic Party

A 3D cooperative casual game

Nombre: Mechanic Party

Desarrollador: FranForge

Periodo: May 2024 - Aug 2024

Tecnologías: Unity, C#, Plastic SCM

Sinopsis: Experience a group of mechanics trying to build their dream workshop by traveling across festivals, earning recognition and progress toward their destiny.

Género y Etiquetas: Cooperative, Arcade, Local multiplayer.
